Manga Recommendations

The best-written manga series

One Piece

One Piece is a story about a group of pirates searching for the ultimate treasure, the One Piece. It's a tale of adventure, friendship, and the pursuit of dreams.

Pinnacle of Fiction.

Berserk

Berserk is a dark, brutal journey through a world that never shows Guts mercy. But beneath all the blood and heartbreak, it's about holding on when everything feels lost.

Pinnacle of Manga.

Monster

Monster follows Dr. Tenma, a kind surgeon whose decision to save a young boy comes back to haunt him when the boy grows into a terrifying killer.

Pinnacle of Psychological Manga.

Haikyuu

Haikyuu!! follows Hinata, a small but fearless volleyball player chasing a huge dream. Every match is a fight, and every point makes you cheer for him.

Pinnacle of Sports Manga.

Your Lie in April

Your Lie in April is a touching story about a piano prodigy who loses his ability to hear music after his mother's death, and the girl who helps him find it again.

Pinnacle of Romantic Drama Manga
